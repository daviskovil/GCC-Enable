## Rules for every post

- **Byline:** `GCC Enable Editorial` (the default; no need to set `author`).
- **Numbers:** use only the sourced industry statistics listed in the README, always with the source line. No invented client stories, quotes or results.
- **Length:** 850+ words of body copy, short paragraphs, 4–6 `##` sections separated by `---` dividers.
- **Required pieces:** banner (`banner` + `bannerAlt` in the front matter), `<KeyTakeaways>` near the top, at least one `<Figure>` diagram, one mid-article `<Cta programme="…" />` and a closing `<Cta />`.
- **Spelling:** Indian English (programme, organisation, centre).
